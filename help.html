Frequently asked

When will I get my proof?

Within 24 working hours of your order, on WhatsApp and email. Printing starts only after you approve it.

How long does delivery take?

Most orders dispatch 2–3 working days after proof approval. Transit is usually 2–5 days depending on your pincode.

Is delivery free?

Free on orders above ₹999. Below that, a small charge is shown at checkout. Branch pickup is always free.

Will I get a GST invoice?

Yes, with every order. Add your GSTIN at checkout to claim input tax credit.

Can I change my order after paying?

Yes, anytime before you approve the proof.

I don't have a design. Can you help?

Yes — choose “Design it for me” on the product page and our designer will set it up from your logo and text.
